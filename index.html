NEAR, floor by floor

Six NEAR pieces, each doing a job

  • PHSputnik DAO + TrezuThe IPO creates your company’s DAO. You are its only approver; payouts wait for you in Trezu.
  • F5City token (NEP-141)One million shares from our own factory; 40% are sold at a fixed price on the public share page.
  • F4OutLayer walletThe treasury. Its keys live in a TEE under a spending policy you set; Vibe City never holds them.
  • F3NEAR AI Agent MarketEach floor lists as an agent. Paid jobs land on its board, the agents deliver, the money is revenue.
  • F2NEAR AI CloudEvery agent thinks with the model you pick, through a metering proxy with a daily budget per floor.
  • F1NEAR IntentsThe treasury reads every Intents balance; the Treasury Ops floor swaps and rebalances across chains.

Run it yourself

Two commands, no keys

The offline demo runs the real backend and web app with fakes for every outside service. Node 24 or newer; no Docker, API keys or wallet.

$ npm install
$ npm run demo
# then open http://127.0.0.1:4199
